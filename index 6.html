Rack layout

Each aisle is set up on its own, so they can differ in how many racks they hold and how tall they are. Bins count straight through an aisle: in aisle R, R1 is the top bin of the first rack, R4 is the floor bin under it, and R5 starts the top of the next rack.

Separate with commas. These fill the dropdown on the put-away form.
